## A Thank-You From Our Family to Yours

Since 2023, our crew has finished more than 1,000 jobs across St. Louis City, St. Louis County and St. Charles County. That number still surprises me when I say it out loud.

But what I think about most isn't the number. It's that every one of those jobs started with someone opening their front door to a family-run crew they'd never met. You let us into your garages, basements, spare rooms and kitchens. That's real trust, and we don't take it lightly.

Thanksgiving is a good time to slow down and say it plainly: thank you. Faith and family come first for us, and getting to do honest work for our neighbors is something I'm grateful for every day.

## Our Thanksgiving Week Hours

We want our crew home with their families for the holiday, so here's the schedule:

- **Monday to Wednesday, Nov. 23 to 25:** open, 7 AM to 5 PM
- **Thursday, Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving):** closed
- **Friday, Nov. 27 (Black Friday):** closed
- **Monday, Nov. 30:** back to work, regular hours

As always, we're closed on weekends. Our regular hours are Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM.

## Getting the House Ready Before Company Comes

A lot of folks look around the week before Thanksgiving and realize the spare room is full of boxes, the garage has no room for anyone to park, or the old couch needs to go before the new one arrives. If that's you, the earlier part of the week is your window.

### What we haul

- **Junk removal:** furniture, appliances, mattresses, household and business junk
- **Cleanouts:** garages, basements, estates, move-outs, whole properties
- **Demolition:** sheds, decks, hot tubs, kitchens, bathrooms and interiors
- **Construction debris:** drywall, lumber, flooring and remodel leftovers

We don't take tires or paint, so set those aside before we arrive.

### How pricing works

We price by the load, from a 1/4 load up to a full 17-yard truck. If it's just a few small items, there's a $125 minimum pickup. Before anything goes on the truck, we confirm your price with you, so there are no surprises. You can see the full breakdown on our [pricing page](/pricing/).

There are no extra fees for mattresses, appliances or other items beyond the load price.

### Where it goes

Our loads go to a landfill facility that sorts and separates materials, so it isn't all just dumped in one pile.
