Every November we hear the same thing from homeowners around St. Louis: "Family's coming, and I can't even get the car in the garage." The garage and the basement are where everything goes when there's no time to deal with it. Then the holidays show up and that space is suddenly needed.

Here's a real garage we cleared out, plus a few practical tips for getting your own space ready before Thanksgiving and Christmas.

## What This Garage Looked Like

This was a one-car garage packed with black trash bags, cardboard, empty luxury vinyl flooring boxes and leftover bits from a remodel. Debris was scattered across the floor, and you couldn't have pulled a car in if you tried.

Our crew loaded it all up and hauled it away. What was left was a clear concrete floor and a garage that's usable again. No sorting piles left behind, no bags waiting for trash day.

## Why the Holidays Sneak Up on Garages and Basements

The garage is the easiest place to put things "for now." Boxes from a project, bags of stuff to deal with later, old furniture that didn't make the move. A few months go by and "for now" becomes a wall of clutter.

The holidays change that quickly. You may need:

- **Parking space** so guests can use the driveway
- **A spot for extra chairs, tables or a cot** for overnight family
- **Room in the basement** for kids to play or for an extra bed
- **Somewhere to stash** the decorations once they come out of storage

## How to Get Your Garage or Basement Ready

### Start with a quick walk-through

Grab a phone and take a few pictures of the space. Decide what's staying, and be honest about the rest. Old project boxes, broken items and things you haven't touched in years usually don't need another season in the garage.

### Pull out what you're keeping

If there are things you want to keep, move them to one side or one corner. That way, everything else can go in one trip, and nobody has to guess what stays.

### Don't wait for the last week

The days right before Thanksgiving and Christmas fill up fast for everyone, including us. We work Monday through Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM, so if you want the space clear before guests show up, book a weekday spot early.

### Let one crew haul it all

You don't need to drag bags to the curb or rent a dumpster that sits in your driveway for a week. Our crew does the lifting and loading, and the loads go to a landfill facility that sorts and separates materials.
